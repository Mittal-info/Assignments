Step 4: Different widths par test karo
📱 Mobile — 375px
Card vertical ho jayega:

IMAGE
  ↓
PRODUCT DETAILS
  ↓
PRICE
  ↓
BUY NOW

Screenshot note:

At 375px, the product card changes to a vertical layout. The image appears on top and the product details appear below it.

📱 Tablet — 768px
Card horizontal rahega, lekin padding aur font size smaller ho jayega.

Screenshot note:

At 768px, the card remains horizontal, but its width, padding, and font sizes are reduced for better readability.

🖥️ Desktop — 1200px+
Card spacious horizontal layout me rahega.

Screenshot note:

At desktop width, the product image and details appear side by side with larger spacing, padding, and font sizes.
